Become a Member

Membership Requirements

We ask that members commit to attending weekly meetings and contributing to at least one active project. There are no fees or dues - our club is entirely student-run and funded through the grace of ICC.

How to Join

Joining De Anza Aerospacs is easy! Simply attend one of our weekly general meetings. We hold new member orientations at the beginning of each quarter where you'll learn about our current projects and how to get involved.

Meeting Time: Thursdays at 5:00 PM
Location: Room S48
