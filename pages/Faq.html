Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to join?
We welcome members of all skill levels. We provide mentorship to help everyone learn and grow.
What majors are welcome?
All majors are welcome! Passion for aerospace is all that's required.
When do you meet?
We typically meet once a week for general meetings. Additional workshop times are available for those working on our two stage launch projects.
How can I get involved?
Simply attend one of our meetings! We also have a new member orientation at the beginning of each quarter.
